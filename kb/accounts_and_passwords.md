# Accounts and passwords

Sample knowledge-base article for the Tobi demo. Replace with your company's real policy.

## Password policy
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or more words is recommended.
Passwords expire every 180 days. You get a reminder 14 days before expiry.
Never reuse a password from another site and never share it, not even with IT.

## Change your password
Press Ctrl+Alt+Delete and choose "Change a password". Type the old password, then the new one twice.
On a company laptop outside the office, connect to the VPN first so the new password syncs.

## Forgotten password or locked account
Tobi cannot reset passwords or unlock accounts: IT must verify your identity first.
Ask Tobi to raise a ticket, or call the service desk. Have your employee ID ready.
An account locks after 10 wrong attempts and unlocks by itself after 30 minutes.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
Use the Microsoft Authenticator app. If you get a sign-in prompt you did not start, choose Deny and report it to IT.
A new phone needs MFA to be set up again: raise a ticket so IT can issue a temporary access pass.


## Forgot your Windows PIN or screen lock
Windows does not use a lock pattern (that is an Android feature). A company laptop is unlocked with your password, a PIN or Windows Hello (face or fingerprint).
If you forgot the PIN, choose "I forgot my PIN" on the sign-in screen and verify with your phone (MFA), then set a new PIN.
If you forgot the account password, use the official self-service reset page (ask Tobi to open it) or call the IT helpdesk. Tobi cannot reset a password or PIN itself.
To add or change a PIN or Windows Hello: Settings > Accounts > Sign-in options.
