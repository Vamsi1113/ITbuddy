# Troubleshooting keyboard, mouse, USB devices, docks and power

## Keyboard or mouse stopped working
What it usually means: dead batteries, a loose cable or receiver, a USB port problem, or a Bluetooth disconnect.
Try this: replace the batteries, reseat the USB receiver or cable in another port, turn Bluetooth off and on, and restart the computer. Test the device on another computer.
Tobi can: list keyboards, mice and USB devices and whether each works, and report devices with a problem.
Call IT when the device works elsewhere but not on your computer.

## Touchpad is not working or moves by itself
What it usually means: it was switched off with a key, a driver issue, or something touching it.
Try this: use the touchpad key (often Fn plus a function key), open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad, and clean it.
Tobi can: open the Mouse and touchpad settings page.

## A USB device is not recognised
What it usually means: a faulty cable or port, not enough power from a hub, or a missing driver.
Try this: use another port and cable, plug straight into the computer, and restart.
Tobi can: list USB devices and report problems.
Call IT when a device needs a driver installed.

## Docking station: screens, network or devices do not work
What it usually means: a stale dock driver, a loose cable, or insufficient power.
Try this: unplug the dock's power for a minute, reconnect it, and restart the computer. Connect to the laptop with the original cable.
Tobi can: check displays, USB devices and the network adapter.
Call IT when it fails after updates for everyone using that model.

## Laptop will not charge or says "plugged in, not charging"
What it usually means: a faulty charger or cable, the wrong power rating, a worn battery, or battery protection settings.
Try this: try another outlet, check the charger light and cable for damage, remove the battery limit setting if the maker's app has one, and restart.
Tobi can: read the battery status.
Call IT when the charger is correct but the battery is not charging.

## Laptop battery drains quickly
What it usually means: high background use, high screen brightness, a worn battery.
Try this: lower the brightness, close heavy programs, use battery saver, and unplug unused USB devices.
Tobi can: read the battery status and the programs using the most memory.

## Computer will not turn on
What it usually means: no power, a flat battery, or a hardware fault.
Try this: check the outlet and the charger, hold the power button for 15 seconds, wait a minute and press it again, and unplug USB devices.
Call IT when the power light stays off: this needs hands-on repair.

## Keys type the wrong characters or the wrong language
What it usually means: the input language or keyboard layout changed.
Try this: press Windows + Space to switch the layout, and check Settings > Time & language > Language & region.
Tobi can: open the Language settings page.

## Screen brightness or volume keys do nothing
What it usually means: the function key mode is switched, or a driver is missing.
Try this: press Fn + Esc (or the Fn Lock key) to switch the mode, and restart.

## Webcam or microphone on a laptop is blocked
What it usually means: a privacy shutter, a hardware switch, or a privacy setting.
Try this: check the shutter and the keyboard camera key, then Settings > Privacy & security.
Tobi can: open the camera and microphone privacy pages.
