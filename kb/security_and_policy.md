# Security and policy

Sample knowledge-base article for the Tobi demo. Replace with your company's real policy.

## Report a suspicious email (phishing)
Do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report" button in Outlook, or forward the email to IT security.
Ask Tobi to "check this link" and paste the link: it checks the address without opening it.
If you already entered your password, tell IT at once so your sessions can be signed out.

## Lost or stolen laptop or phone
Tell IT immediately, at any hour. IT locks and locates the device and signs out your sessions.
Ask Tobi to raise a P1 security ticket.

## Personal devices (BYOD)
Personal phones may use company email only through the Outlook app with a device PIN.
Personal laptops may not connect to the internal network.

## USB sticks and external drives
Only encrypted company USB drives may hold company data. Unknown USB sticks must never be plugged in.

## Acceptable use
Company devices are for work. Do not install unapproved software, switch off the antivirus or firewall,
or share your account. Tobi refuses such requests and opens a ticket instead.
