# Printers and devices

Sample knowledge-base article for the Tobi demo. Replace with your company's real guidance.

## Add an office printer
Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners and choose "Add device".
Office printers are named by floor, for example "PRN-Floor2-Color". Pick the one nearest to you.

## Document stuck in the print queue
Ask Tobi to "clear my print queue". It cancels the waiting documents after you approve.
Then switch the printer off and on, and print again.

## Headset or microphone not working in calls
Check that the headset is selected in the call app under Settings > Devices.
In Windows, open Settings > System > Sound and choose the headset as input and output.
Check Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone: access must be On.

## Second monitor not detected
Press Windows+P and choose "Extend". Unplug the cable or dock for ten seconds and plug it in again.
Docking stations need their own power supply to be connected.

## Request new hardware
Keyboards, mice and headsets are stock items: ask Tobi to raise a hardware request ticket.
Laptops and monitors need your manager's approval; IT asks for it after the ticket is opened.
A loan laptop is available at the service desk while yours is repaired.
