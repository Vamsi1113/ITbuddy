# How to: everyday Windows settings

## Take a screenshot
Steps: press **Win + Shift + S** and drag a box: the picture goes to the clipboard, paste it with Ctrl + V. **Print Screen** copies the whole screen. **Win + Print Screen** saves a full-screen picture in Pictures > Screenshots. The **Snipping Tool** app also records the screen on Windows 11.
Tobi can: open the Snipping Tool.

## Record the screen
Steps: open the **Snipping Tool**, switch to the video camera icon, select **New**, drag the area and select **Start**. Stop with the red button, then save the video. Xbox Game Bar (**Win + G**) can record one app window.

## Lock the computer when you walk away
Steps: press **Win + L**. To lock automatically, open Settings > Accounts > Sign-in options and set **Dynamic lock** or a short screen-saver time.

## Change screen resolution, size of text and apps (scaling)
Steps: Settings > System > **Display**. Under "Scale & layout" choose the **recommended** resolution and change **Scale** (for example 125%) to make everything bigger or smaller.
Tobi can: open the Display settings.

## Use two screens or a projector
Steps: plug in the screen, then press **Win + P** and choose **Duplicate** (same picture), **Extend** (one big desktop) or **Second screen only**. Arrange and identify screens in Settings > System > Display. Make one screen the main one with **Make this my main display**.

## Night light, dark mode and brightness
Steps: night light: Settings > System > Display > **Night light**. Dark mode: Settings > Personalization > Colors > **Choose your mode** > Dark. Brightness: the slider in Quick Settings (Win + A) or the laptop brightness keys.
Tobi can: open the Colors settings or switch dark mode on or off after you approve.

## Choose which speaker or headset plays sound
Steps: select the speaker icon near the clock (Win + A) > the arrow next to the volume slider > pick the device. All devices: Settings > System > **Sound**.
Tobi can: open the Sound settings.

## Turn notifications off for a while (Do not disturb / Focus)
Steps: Settings > System > Notifications > **Do not disturb**, or select the clock and start a **Focus** session. Per-app notifications can be switched off in the same page.
Tobi can: open the Notifications settings.

## Change the default app for a file type or links (browser, PDF reader)
Steps: Settings > Apps > **Default apps**: search for the app and choose which file types it opens, or set a default browser. Quick way: right-click a file > **Open with** > **Choose another app** > tick **Always**.
Tobi can: open the Default apps settings.

## Add a keyboard language or switch between languages
Steps: Settings > Time & language > **Language & region** > **Add a language**. Switch with **Win + Space** or the language letters near the clock.
Tobi can: open the Language settings.

## Clipboard history (paste something you copied earlier)
Steps: press **Win + V**. The first time, select **Turn on**. Pin items you use often.
Tobi can: turn clipboard history on after you approve.

## Arrange windows side by side (Snap) and virtual desktops
Steps: drag a window to the screen edge or press **Win + Left/Right arrow**. On Windows 11 hover over the maximise button for layouts. New desktop: **Win + Ctrl + D**, switch with **Win + Ctrl + Left/Right**, see all with **Win + Tab**.

## Pin an app to the taskbar or Start
Steps: open Start, find the app, right-click it > **Pin to taskbar** or **Pin to Start**. Unpin the same way.

## Change the desktop or lock screen picture
Steps: Settings > Personalization > **Background** or **Lock screen**. Company devices may set these for you.
Tobi can: open the Personalization settings.

## Set the date, time and time zone
Steps: Settings > Time & language > **Date & time**: turn on **Set time automatically** and **Set time zone automatically**, then **Sync now**.
Tobi can: check whether the clock is correct and open the Date & time settings.

## Power, sleep and lid settings
Steps: Settings > System > **Power & battery** (Windows 11) or Power & sleep: choose when the screen turns off and when the PC sleeps. Lid and power button: Control Panel > Power Options > **Choose what closing the lid does**. **Battery saver** stretches battery life.
Tobi can: open the Power settings and show the battery level.

## Find the computer name, Windows version and serial number
Steps: Settings > System > **About** shows the device name and Windows edition and version. Type **winver** in Start for the exact build.
Tobi can: show the computer name, model, serial number, Windows version and build, and last restart time.

## Make text bigger, use the magnifier or high contrast
Steps: Settings > Accessibility: **Text size**, **Magnifier** (Win + Plus to zoom, Win + Esc to stop), **Contrast themes**, **Narrator** (Ctrl + Win + Enter).
Tobi can: open the Accessibility settings.

## Useful keyboard shortcuts
Win + E File Explorer, Win + I Settings, Win + D show desktop, Win + L lock, Alt + Tab switch apps, Ctrl + Shift + Esc Task Manager, Win + . emoji, Win + H voice typing, Win + X quick menu (Disk Management, Device Manager, Terminal).
