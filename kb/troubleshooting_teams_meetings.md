# Troubleshooting Teams, Zoom and online meetings

## Others cannot hear me in a meeting
What it usually means: the wrong microphone is selected, the microphone is muted by the app or by a hardware switch, or the app has no permission to use it.
Try this: in the meeting open the audio settings and pick the right microphone, check the mute button and any mute key on the headset, then open Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone and make sure apps may use it. Test with the Windows Sound settings input meter.
Tobi can: list your audio devices and whether each is working, and open the microphone privacy and Sound settings pages.
Call IT when the microphone does not appear in any list.

## I cannot hear others in a meeting
What it usually means: the wrong speaker is selected, volume is down or muted, or Bluetooth connected to a different device.
Try this: select the right speaker in the meeting settings, check volume in the taskbar, and reconnect the headset.
Tobi can: list the audio devices and open the Sound settings page.

## Camera not working or shows a black screen
What it usually means: another program is using the camera, a privacy switch or shutter is closed, the wrong camera is selected, or the app lacks permission.
Try this: close other programs that may hold the camera, open the shutter or camera key, choose the camera in the meeting settings, and check Settings > Privacy & security > Camera.
Tobi can: list cameras and whether they work, and open the camera privacy page.
Call IT when the camera is not listed at all.

## Echo, feedback or robotic sound
What it usually means: two devices pick up the same audio, or the network is weak.
Try this: use a headset instead of speakers, only one device per room in a meeting, mute when not talking, and move closer to the Wi-Fi.
Tobi can: check the connection quality.

## Video or audio freezes and drops
What it usually means: weak Wi-Fi, a busy network, a slow computer, or a VPN adding delay.
Try this: turn off your own video, close heavy programs, move closer to the router or use a cable, and leave the VPN if policy allows.
Tobi can: measure the connection and the computer's load.

## Teams is slow, crashes or shows a blank window
What it usually means: a damaged cache, too little memory, or an old version.
Try this: close Teams fully, clear the Teams cache, and start it again. Update Teams and restart the computer.
Tobi can: clear the Teams cache after you approve, and show recent Teams crashes.
Call IT when Teams will not sign in after clearing the cache.

## Teams sign-in loops or says you are signed out
What it usually means: an expired sign-in, MFA not completed, a wrong clock, or a conflict with another account.
Try this: sign out of Teams and Office, check the clock, sign in again and approve the MFA request.
Tobi can: check the clock and how long until your password expires.
Call IT when the account is blocked.

## Screen sharing shows a black or blank screen
What it usually means: sharing a protected window, a graphics driver problem, or sharing an app that is minimised.
Try this: share the whole screen instead of one window, restart the app, and update the graphics driver through Windows Update.
Call IT when a particular app always shows black.

## Cannot join a meeting link
What it usually means: the link is expired or wrong, your account does not have access, or the browser blocks the app.
Try this: open the link in a browser instead of the app, sign in with the right account, and ask the organiser to resend.
Tobi can: check that the website opens and the connection works.

## Zoom will not start or install
What it usually means: a blocked installer, no admin rights, or an old version.
Try this: join from the browser link instead, or ask Tobi to install it.
Tobi can: check whether Zoom is installed and, if it is on the approved list, install it after you approve.
