# Troubleshooting accounts, passwords, sign-in and access

## What Tobi can and cannot do with accounts
Tobi never resets a password, unlocks an account or changes multi-factor authentication itself, because those need your identity to be verified. It can open your organisation's official self-service page where you verify with MFA and set the new password yourself, check how many days remain before your password expires, tell you which account you are signed in as, and raise a ticket for IT.

## I forgot my password or I am locked out
What it usually means: the password was typed wrongly too many times, it expired, or it was changed on another device and this one still has the old one.
Try this: use the self-service password reset page (ask Tobi to open it), verify with MFA, and choose a new password. Wait about 30 minutes after too many failed attempts: many accounts unlock by themselves.
Tobi can: open the password reset page for you and raise a ticket if the page does not work.
Call IT when self-service is not set up for your account.

## My password expired or expires soon
What it usually means: passwords expire on a schedule (see the password policy).
Try this: press Ctrl+Alt+Delete, choose Change a password, and on a laptop outside the office connect to the VPN first so the new password syncs.
Tobi can: show how many days remain until your password expires.

## MFA prompt does not arrive or approval fails
What it usually means: no signal on the phone, notifications are off, the time on the phone is wrong, or the authenticator app was reinstalled.
Try this: open the authenticator app and approve from inside it, check the phone's date and time, turn on notifications, and choose "use a code" instead.
Call IT: re-registering MFA needs identity verification. Tobi can raise the ticket.

## Windows PIN or fingerprint (Windows Hello) not working
What it usually means: a security update, a changed device, or a damaged setup. Windows has no pattern lock: it uses a password, PIN or Windows Hello.
Try this: on the sign-in screen choose "I forgot my PIN" and verify, then set a new PIN. Add or change them under Settings > Accounts > Sign-in options.
Tobi can: open the Sign-in options settings page.
Call IT when the PIN reset page cannot verify you.

## Access denied to a shared folder, SharePoint site or application
What it usually means: you do not have permission yet, or the permission is on another account, or you are not on the VPN.
Try this: check you are signed in with your work account, connect the VPN, and open the link again. Ask the owner of the folder to grant access.
Tobi can: raise an access request ticket with the exact location.
Call IT or the owner: permissions are granted by the owner or by IT.

## Cannot sign in to a work app or website
What it usually means: an expired sign-in, a blocked account, a wrong account chosen, or a browser keeping an old session.
Try this: sign out fully, close the browser, sign in again with the work account, and try a private window.
Tobi can: check the connection, the clock and your password status.

## The account is blocked or disabled
What it usually means: too many failed attempts, a security block after unusual activity, or an account change.
Try this: do not keep retrying. Call or raise a ticket.
Call IT: only IT can unblock it, after verifying who you are.

## Do I have administrator rights?
What it usually means: most users have standard rights, which is intended for safety.
Tobi can: tell you whether you are an administrator. Requests for administrator rights need approval from IT: Tobi can raise the request.

## Your account is shown in another country or you received a sign-in alert
What it usually means: someone else may have your password, or it is a VPN or travel.
Try this: change your password at once through self-service and tell IT.
Tobi can: raise a security ticket with high priority.
