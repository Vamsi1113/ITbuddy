# Troubleshooting sound, headsets, Bluetooth, monitors and displays

## No sound from the speakers or headset
What it usually means: volume is muted or very low, the wrong output device is selected, or the device is not connected.
Try this: check the volume and the mute key, click the speaker icon in the taskbar and pick the right output, replug the headset, and restart the computer.
Tobi can: list audio devices and whether each works, open the Sound settings page, and check devices Windows reports a problem with.
Call IT when the audio device does not appear anywhere.

## Headset or microphone is not recognised
What it usually means: a loose connector, the wrong port (a headset needs the combined jack), a driver problem, or Windows privacy settings blocking the microphone.
Try this: plug in firmly, try another port, restart the computer, and check Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.
Tobi can: list audio and USB devices and report devices with a problem.

## Bluetooth device will not pair or keeps disconnecting
What it usually means: Bluetooth is off, the device is not in pairing mode, the battery is low, or an old pairing is stuck.
Try this: switch Bluetooth on in Settings, put the device in pairing mode, remove the old pairing and add it again, and charge the device.
Tobi can: open the Bluetooth settings page and list Bluetooth devices and their status.
Call IT when Bluetooth is missing in Settings.

## Second monitor shows "No signal" or is not detected
What it usually means: a loose cable, the wrong input on the monitor, the wrong projection mode, or a dock problem.
Try this: check both ends of the cable, choose the right input on the monitor's menu, press the Windows key + P and pick Extend, and try the cable directly instead of the dock.
Tobi can: list the monitors Windows sees and open the Display settings page.
Call IT when the monitor works on another computer but not yours.

## Screen is blurry, stretched or the wrong size
What it usually means: the resolution or scaling is wrong, or the monitor is not at its native resolution.
Try this: open Settings > System > Display, choose the recommended resolution, and set scaling to the recommended value.
Tobi can: open the Display settings page.

## Screen is black, flickers or goes dark
What it usually means: a loose cable, a graphics driver problem, power saving, or an overheating laptop.
Try this: press Windows + Ctrl + Shift + B to restart the graphics driver, check the cable, increase brightness, and restart.
Call IT when it keeps happening.

## Microphone is too quiet or too loud
What it usually means: the input level or a boost setting is wrong.
Try this: open the Sound settings, choose the input, test it and adjust the level.
Tobi can: open the Sound settings page.

## Camera not listed
What it usually means: a privacy switch, a disabled device, or a driver problem.
Try this: check the shutter and the camera key, then Settings > Privacy & security > Camera.
Tobi can: list cameras and whether they work.
Call IT when no camera is listed.

## External speakers crackle or hum
What it usually means: a poor connection, a loose cable, interference or a USB power issue.
Try this: reseat the cable, move it away from power cables, and try another port.
