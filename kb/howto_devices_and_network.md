# How to: devices and network setup

## Pair a Bluetooth headset, mouse or keyboard
Steps: put the device in pairing mode (usually hold its power or Bluetooth button until a light flashes). Settings > Bluetooth & devices > **Add device** > **Bluetooth** > select it. To fix a bad connection, **Remove device** and pair again.
Tobi can: open the Bluetooth settings.

## Set up a headset or microphone for calls
Steps: plug it in, then pick it in Settings > System > **Sound** for both Output and Input. In Teams: your picture > Settings > **Devices**, choose the headset and **Make a test call**.

## Allow apps to use the camera or microphone
Steps: Settings > Privacy & security > **Camera** (or **Microphone**): turn on access and the switch for the app. Many laptops also have a camera shutter or a mute key.
Tobi can: open the Camera or Microphone privacy settings.

## Change mouse speed, buttons or touchpad gestures
Steps: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > **Mouse** (speed, primary button, scrolling) or **Touchpad** (tap to click, three-finger gestures).
Tobi can: open the Mouse settings.

## Scan a document
Steps: put the page on the scanner, open the **Windows Scan** app (or the multifunction printer's own app), pick the scanner and file type, select **Scan**. Office printers often scan to email directly from their touch screen.

## Set up a docking station
Steps: connect the dock to the laptop with its one cable (usually USB-C or Thunderbolt), plug screens, keyboard, mouse and network into the dock, then use **Win + P** > **Extend**. If something does not appear, unplug the dock for 10 seconds and plug it back in.

## Connect to a Wi-Fi network or forget an old one
Steps: select the network icon near the clock > the arrow next to Wi-Fi > pick the network > **Connect**. To forget: Settings > Network & internet > Wi-Fi > **Manage known networks** > **Forget**.
Tobi can: show the Wi-Fi network, signal strength and speed, and open the Wi-Fi settings.

## Use your phone as a hotspot
Steps: turn on the hotspot on your phone, then connect to it like any Wi-Fi network. Connect the company VPN afterwards for internal sites. Watch your mobile data use.

## Set a connection as metered (save data)
Steps: Settings > Network & internet > Wi-Fi > your network > turn on **Metered connection**. Windows then postpones large downloads and updates.

## Airplane mode
Steps: press the airplane key or open Quick Settings (Win + A) > **Airplane mode**. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth can be switched back on separately while it is on.

## Clear the DNS cache (flushdns)
What it is: Windows remembers website addresses; clearing that list helps after a site moved or a network change.
Steps: Command Prompt: `ipconfig /flushdns`.
Tobi can: clear the DNS cache after you approve and test that sites resolve again.

## Test whether a website or server can be reached
Steps: Command Prompt: `ping example.com` shows whether it answers; `tracert example.com` shows where it stops; `nslookup example.com` checks the name lookup.
Tobi can: test the internet connection, DNS, one website, a server and port, and call quality.

## Connect to a remote computer (Remote Desktop)
Steps: open Start, type **Remote Desktop Connection** (mstsc), type the computer name, select **Connect** and sign in with your work account. From home connect the VPN first.
Call IT when you need access to a computer you have not used before.

## Add a second user language for voice typing or dictation
Steps: press **Win + H** in any text box to start voice typing. Pick the language in Settings > Time & language > **Speech**.
