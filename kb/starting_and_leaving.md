# Joining, moving and leaving

Sample knowledge-base article for the Tobi demo. Replace with your company's real process.

## New starter checklist
On day one you receive a laptop, your account name and a temporary access pass.
Sign in, set a password, set up MFA with the Authenticator app, then connect to "Corp-Secure" Wi-Fi.
Your manager requests extra app access for you through a ticket.

## Changing team or role
Your new manager raises an access request ticket. Old access that you no longer need is removed after 30 days.

## Leaving the company
Return the laptop, charger, badge and any loan devices to IT on your last day.
Your account is disabled at the end of your last working day. Move personal files off the laptop before then.
Your manager decides who receives your mailbox and files.
