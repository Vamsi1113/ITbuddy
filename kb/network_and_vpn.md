# Network, Wi-Fi and VPN

Sample knowledge-base article for the Tobi demo. Replace with your company's real guidance.

## Connect to office Wi-Fi
Choose the network "Corp-Secure" and sign in with your company email and password.
Guests use "Corp-Guest"; the guest password is shown at reception and changes weekly.

## Connect to the VPN
Open the VPN app from the system tray, choose the gateway nearest to you and select Connect.
Approve the MFA prompt on your phone. The VPN is needed for file shares, the intranet and internal apps.

## VPN keeps disconnecting
Check that the Wi-Fi signal is above 60 percent. Move closer to the router or use a network cable.
Home routers older than five years often drop VPN tunnels: restart the router once.
If it still drops, ask Tobi to raise a ticket and mention the time of the last disconnect.

## Slow or broken video calls
Close other apps that use the network, such as cloud backups and video streaming.
Use a cable instead of Wi-Fi when you can. Turn off your camera if sound breaks up.
Ask Tobi to "check my connection quality for calls" to measure delay and jitter.

## Hotel or public Wi-Fi sign-in page does not appear
Open a browser and go to http://neverssl.com to force the sign-in page. Connect the VPN after you are signed in.
