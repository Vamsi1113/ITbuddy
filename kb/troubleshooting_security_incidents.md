# Troubleshooting security warnings and suspicious activity

## What to do first with any security worry
Stay calm and do not keep clicking. Do not enter your password. Disconnect from the network only if told to. Tell IT at once with what you saw: the message, the website or sender, and what you clicked. Early reports prevent damage, and nobody is in trouble for reporting.
Tobi can: raise a high-priority security ticket with what it found, check your antivirus and firewall status, look for recent threats, check a link without opening it, and start a quick scan after you approve.

## I clicked a suspicious link or opened an attachment
What it usually means: it may be phishing or malware.
Try this: close the page, do not enter anything, run a quick scan, and report it. If you typed your password, change it through self-service and tell IT immediately so your sessions can be signed out.
Tobi can: start a quick antivirus scan after you approve and raise a priority ticket.

## Is this link or email safe?
What it usually means: look for a sender address that is slightly wrong, urgent or threatening wording, unexpected attachments, and links whose real address differs from the text.
Try this: do not click. Ask Tobi to check the link: it examines the address without opening it.
Tobi can: check a link for signs of phishing such as look-alike domains and odd domain endings.

## Antivirus shows a threat or says protection is off
What it usually means: Windows Security found something or a setting was changed.
Try this: open Windows Security and follow its steps, and do not switch protection off.
Tobi can: report the antivirus and firewall status and recent threats. It never switches protection off.
Call IT when protection cannot be turned on or a threat is not removed.

## A pop-up says my computer is infected or tells me to call a number
What it usually means: it is a scam page, not a real warning.
Try this: close the browser tab or end the browser from the Task Manager, do not call the number or download anything, and clear the browser cache.
Tobi can: clear the browser cache after you approve and run a quick scan.

## Ransom note, files renamed or locked
What it usually means: ransomware.
Try this: disconnect from the network (unplug the cable or turn Wi-Fi off), do not restart, and call IT at once by phone.
Tobi can: raise the highest-priority security ticket.

## I lost my laptop or phone, or it was stolen
Tell IT immediately at any hour. IT locks and locates the device and signs out your sessions. Change your password through self-service from another device.
Tobi can: raise a P1 security ticket.

## Someone asked for my password or a code
Never share your password or an MFA code, not with IT either. IT will never ask for them. Report the request.
Tobi can: raise a security ticket.

## USB sticks and external drives
Never plug in an unknown USB stick. Only company-approved encrypted drives may hold company data.

## Is my firewall on?
Tobi can: check that the firewall and antivirus are on and tell you plainly. Switching them off is never done by Tobi.
