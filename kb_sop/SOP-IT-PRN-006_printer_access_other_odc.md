# Using printers of another ODC

## Scope

Applies to every employee and contractor in every ODC. Each ODC's printers belong to that ODC and are set up for the
people who work there (see the printing SOP of your own ODC).

## Using another ODC's printers

- Printers of another ODC (for example the side block or another delivery centre) may not be used without approval.
- Raise a request in ServiceNow: Request > Printer access (other ODC). Give the ODC, the printer if you know it, and your reason.
- The request is approved by your reporting manager and by the IT owner of that ODC. It normally takes 2 working days.
- You can also email the IT Service Desk of that ODC with your ODC, floor, seat and reason. Do not email on behalf of someone else.
- Access is given for a period and reviewed. It is withdrawn when the need ends.

## ODCs that do not allow outside use

Client-dedicated ODCs (the ODC's own printing SOP says so) do not give printer access to people from other ODCs, and no
request is raised. Print on the printers of your own ODC instead.

## Until access is approved

- Use the approved printers of your own ODC, and the scanners and other devices listed for it.
- Save the document as a PDF and ask the ODC's IT Service Desk about releasing it, only if they agree.
- Never connect a printer of another ODC yourself, and never use another person's badge or account to print.
