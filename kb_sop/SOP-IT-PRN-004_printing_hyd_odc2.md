# Printing in Hyderabad ODC 2

## Scope

This SOP applies to every employee and contractor working from Hyderabad ODC 2 (HYD-ODC-2), on any company laptop or
virtual desktop. Personal devices must not print to ODC printers.

## Approved printers

Only these printers may be used in HYD-ODC-2:

| Printer | Location | Type | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HYD2-F3-Mono-01 | 3rd floor, near the pantry | Laser, duplex | Black and white |
| HYD2-F3-Colour-02 | 3rd floor, print room | Laser, duplex | Colour |
| HYD2-F5-Mono-03 | 5th floor, east wing | Laser, duplex | Black and white |

Printers are added from the print server `\\printsrv-hyd`. Do not install printer drivers from the internet.

## Accepted file types and limits

- Accepted: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), plain text (.txt), PNG and JPG images.
- Not accepted: executables, archives (.zip, .rar), audio and video files.
- Maximum 100 pages per job on mono printers and 30 pages on the colour printer.
- Jobs over these limits need approval from your reporting manager before printing.

## Colour printing

Colour printing is only for client deliverables and marketing material. It is allowed on HYD2-F3-Colour-02 only and
requires membership of the HYD2-Colour-Print group. Everyone else prints in black and white.

## Confidential documents

Documents labelled Confidential or Highly Confidential must be printed with Secure Release: the job is held at the
printer until you tap your ID badge. Never print Highly Confidential documents to the colour printer. Collect printouts
immediately and shred unwanted copies in the locked bins.

## Getting help

Raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk for printer faults, access to the colour group, or approvals over the page
limits.
