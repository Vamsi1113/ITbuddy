# Wi-Fi and VPN in Hyderabad ODC 2

## Wi-Fi networks

- Company laptops connect to HYD2-Corp. Sign-in uses the laptop's certificate, no password is typed.
- Personal phones and visitors may use HYD2-Guest only. HYD2-Guest cannot reach internal systems or printers.
- Never connect a company laptop to a personal hotspot or to HYD2-Guest while handling company data.

## VPN

- In the office (HYD-ODC-2) the VPN is not needed.
- When working from home or outside the ODC, always connect GlobalProtect to vpn-hyd.example.com before opening
  internal systems.
- Report VPN faults to the IT Service Desk with the time of the failure.

## Wired network

Desk ports on floors 3 and 5 are for company devices only. Do not plug in personal switches or routers.
