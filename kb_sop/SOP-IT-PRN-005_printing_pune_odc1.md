# Printing in Pune ODC 1

## Scope

This SOP applies to everyone working from Pune ODC 1 (PUNE-ODC-1). Pune ODC 1 is a client-dedicated delivery centre
with stricter printing controls than other ODCs.

## Approved printers

Only one printer may be used in PUNE-ODC-1:

| Printer | Location | Type | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PUN1-G-SecurePrint-01 | Ground floor, security desk | Laser, duplex | Black and white |

There is no colour printing in PUNE-ODC-1.

## Accepted file types and limits

- Accepted: PDF only. Convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint files to PDF before printing.
- Maximum 25 pages per job.
- Every print job needs approval from your project manager, raised as a ServiceNow request.

## Confidential documents

All printing in PUNE-ODC-1 uses Secure Release with badge tap, whatever the document's label. Client documents must not
leave the ODC: printed client material is logged at the security desk.
