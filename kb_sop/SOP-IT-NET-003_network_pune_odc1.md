# Wi-Fi and VPN in Pune ODC 1

## Wi-Fi networks

- Only company devices may connect, and only to PUN1-Secure.
- There is no guest Wi-Fi in Pune ODC 1. Personal phones must stay off the network and be kept in the lockers.

## VPN

- In the ODC the VPN is not needed.
- Working from home needs the client-approved VPN profile and your project manager's approval. Without approval do not
  connect to client systems from outside the ODC.

## Wired network

Desk ports on the delivery floor are locked to registered devices. A device that is not registered gets no network.
